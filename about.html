Welcome to Jagannath Puri Travel Point

Your Trusted Local Travel Partner in Puri

Jagannath Puri Travel Point is your one-stop destination for all travel needs in Puri and across Odisha. Run by Ashok, a local travel expert based on Marine Drive Road, Swargadwar Road, Puri, we provide personalized travel assistance to make your trip comfortable and memorable.

Whether you're planning a spiritual pilgrimage to the sacred Jagannath Temple, a scenic day trip to Chilika Lake, or simply need help with hotel bookings and ticket reservations, Ashok and the team are here to help — every day from 4:00 AM to 11:00 PM.

We believe in honest pricing, transparent service, and ensuring every traveller feels at home in Puri. All bookings are enquiry-based with actual rates confirmed before booking.
